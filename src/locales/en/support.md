# SUPPORT — SEIALZ

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

Seialz is a CRM and Sales Ops platform operated by **Kairos Labs LLC**. This page lists the official support channels for customers, users, and partners.

## Official channel

All support is handled by email:

**[suporte@seialz.com](mailto:suporte@seialz.com)**

We reply within **24 hours** on business days. Requests received on weekends and holidays are answered on the next business day.

## What to include in your email

To speed things up, include in your first message:

1. **Name and company** associated with your Seialz account.
2. **Login email** used to access the platform.
3. **Description of the issue or question**, with as much detail as possible.
4. **Where it happened** — module or screen (for example: Opportunities, Messages, Inbox, Settings).
5. **When it happened** — approximate date and time.
6. **Screenshots or a short video**, if the issue is visual or intermittent.

Never send passwords, API tokens, or integration keys by email. If we need any credential, we will guide you through a secure way to share it.

## Topics we cover

- Questions about using the platform and its features.
- Technical failures, screen errors, and unexpected behavior.
- Integrations (WhatsApp, telephony, payments, and other connectors).
- Access, users, permissions, and organizations.
- Billing, plans, and invoices.
- Sales and partnership requests.

## Privacy and personal data

Requests related to personal data — access, correction, or deletion — should be sent to **[privacidade@seialz.com](mailto:privacidade@seialz.com)**. See also our [Privacy Policy](https://seialz.com/en/privacy-policy) and [Data Deletion Instructions](https://seialz.com/en/data-deletion).

## Urgent incidents

For full platform outages or interruptions in sending and receiving messages, email **[suporte@seialz.com](mailto:suporte@seialz.com)** with a subject line starting with "**URGENT**". These cases are prioritized in the support queue.
